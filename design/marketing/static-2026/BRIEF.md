# ADHD.ME static campaign system

User scope: static only. No video, motion overlays or B-roll deliverables. Keep all earlier game work and main-release requirements active.

Direction: refine the supplied yellow me. identity with floating ADHD letters. Separate campaign identity from the app navigation wordmark. Sunflower yellow, near-black, ivory and a small vermilion square. Confident editorial hierarchy and photographs of everyday people, with clear space for typesetting. Avoid clinical stock-photo cliches and diagnostic claims.

Deliverables to complete and inspect:
- Rerendered master, transparent mark, light/dark and monochrome logo variants, scalable vector sources and export rules.
- A coordinated photo library: individual everyday routines, supportive conversation, study/work, outdoors, and tactile still life. Generated people are fictional, never represented as team members, clinicians or testimonials.
- Square/portrait social posts, static stories, coherent carousel, campaign covers.
- Pamphlet, information sheet, poster, training-program handouts.
- Slide backgrounds, section dividers, image layouts and editable diagrams.
- Newsletter headers, feature banners, article covers, magazine layouts.
- Static name bars, speaker cards and printable badges. Editable name fields are templates, not invented staff credentials.
- Usage guide, research references, prompts, asset manifest, sizes, typography and provenance; ready-to-use exports plus editable sources.

Current generated originals: identity/brand-master-yellow.png and photography/morning-note.png. Generated using built-in image_gen. No competitor artwork is distributed in this pack.

Release gate: inspect every exported layout for correct logo, legible text, clipped faces, typography and margins; check referenced files and image decode; record print-size assumptions. Do not call a digital RGB export press-ready CMYK. No invented user counts, credentials, clinical effects or testimonials. Keep provenance in the handover, not stamped across the campaign artwork.
