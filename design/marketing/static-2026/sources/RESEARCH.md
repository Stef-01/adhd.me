# Campaign research — 27 September 2026

Primary campaign and design sources inspected online:

1. [Alma: Better with people](https://helloalma.com/cg/better-with-people/). Everyday situations and supportive conversation lead the campaign; the service steps sit alongside human imagery. Apply to ADHD.ME: show a specific ordinary moment, then one clear next action. Do not borrow their slogan, outcome claims, photographs or therapist identities.
2. [Lenus identity by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/lenus). The identity combines lifestyle photography, graphic language, palette and voice across digital and physical uses. Apply: one coherent yellow/ink system with quiet, tactile photographs rather than unrelated image treatments.
3. [Alma marketing collateral by Pam Chuy](https://www.pamelachuy.com/work/alma). The designer describes customer/provider communication, launches, print, events and a brand book. Apply: create reusable systems across channels, not only hero posters.
4. [Headspace press and media](https://www.headspace.com/press-and-media). Its press-kit inventory names photography/bios, logo pack, fact sheet and app screenshots. Apply: separate identity files, original photos, product captures and factual copy in the handover. No invented staff bios or company statistics.
5. [Cabinet Health by Otherway](https://www.otherway.com/work/cabinet-health). A consistent consumer health identity across touchpoints offers an adjacent reference for confidence and recognisability. Do not import sustainability or medical claims.

Image-search review included the Lenus portrait campaign, Lenus social stories and Alma conversation/search imagery. Competitor imagery is reference-only, not included in deliverables. Agent Reach's listed command backends were unavailable in this environment; built-in web search and page/image results were used instead.

## Derived pack architecture

Identity masters + colour/type rules; people and scene photo masters; social formats and carousel; newsletter/editorial formats; pamphlet/poster/info sheet; training worksheet/program cover; presentation backgrounds/diagrams; static speaker/name assets; manifest and editable layout sources. This list is our synthesis of the brief and the cited reference systems, not a quotation of a universal campaign standard.

All photographic people in this pack are AI-generated fictional subjects. They must not be labelled as actual patients, practitioners, staff or testimonial authors. No cultural identity, Country, diagnosis or qualification is asserted from appearance. Use consented real portraits when naming actual speakers or providers.
