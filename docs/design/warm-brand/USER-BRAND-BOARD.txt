Prefer --ink #191a17 for titles and lower-thirds. It is legal, it is the product's own dark, and it is the ground the tables in §05 are measured against.

Type over footage
White on --ink. Where type sits over footage rather than a solid, put an --ink scrim under it — never trust footage luminance.

Accent in motion
--on-band #e1a459 is the amber for video, not --accent. On a dark ground --accent is 2.96:1 and disappears.

Lower third
Ground #191a17 at 85–92%, rule in --on-band, name in white, role in --line at 12.86:1 — never --muted, which is 2.45:1 there.

The band on screen
It survives a crop. It does not survive being rebuilt by hand — take the stops from :root, listed in §03.

Chroma
No brand colour troubles a 4:2:0 subsample. The most saturated is --chart-holdout, and that is a data colour that should not appear in motion graphics at all.

Black is not --ink as converted
#191a17 converts to 4/0/12/90 — a 106% black that looks thin and brownish across a large area. Type under 24pt: 100% K alone. Large solid areas: a rich black of 60/40/40/100. Register the two consciously; don't mix them on one page.

Total ink limits
The table is coated-stock advice. Cap at 260% on uncoated and 240% on newsprint. At those limits --accent-deep is fine — a rich black over an accent panel is not. Don't build ink; knock out.

The band in print
A gradient from warm orange to cool periwinkle crosses the whole CMYK space and will band on a cheap press. Under 200 lpi, use the two stops as flat fields with a hard edge, or drop the band and use --accent as a flat rule. A muddy gradient is worse than none.

Spot colour
If a job runs spot, --accent is the one to buy. Ask the printer to match to the hex — do not accept a Pantone number chosen from a screen, including from this page. A spot match is made against a physical book under D50.

Reversed type
White out of --accent at 8pt fills in on uncoated. Reverse only at 10pt and above, and only out of --accent or darker.

Hairlines
--line at 18% ink is close to nothing. Below 0.5pt it will not hold. Where a rule must survive, it is --line-strong.

08 — Typography

Two faces, one contrast axis
Unchanged by this pass, restated so this board stands alone. Both are open-licence — install them for print and video rather than substituting.

Newsreader

Display
Weight ~430, letter-spacing: -0.035em, line-height: 0.99. Reserved for questions asked of the patient. Clamp ceiling in use is clamp(42px, 11vw, 54px); stay at or under ~96px anywhere. Fallback: Georgia, serif.

Inter

UI and body
Everything functional. Fallback: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif. Use tabular-nums wherever digits line up in a column — every measurement on this page does.

Do not add a third family. The pairing already sits on a genuine contrast axis — a display serif against a geometric-humanist sans. A third face does not add an axis; it adds noise.
