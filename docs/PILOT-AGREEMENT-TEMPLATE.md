# Meherr Pilot Agreement — Template Skeleton (W47, v1)

> **DRAFT STRUCTURE ONLY — not legal advice, not a contract.** This is the negotiation-ready
> skeleton of what the pilot agreement must cover; an Australian commercial lawyer turns it into
> the executable document before any practice signs (founder gate G4 prerequisite).

1. **Parties & term** — Meherr entity; the practice; 12-week term + week-0 setup; either
   party may pause sends immediately, terminate on 7 days' notice.
2. **Service description** — availability-invitation service per the Pilot Playbook
   (docs/PILOT-PLAYBOOK.md, incorporated by reference), including the holdout design. The
   practice acknowledges the holdout is integral to measurement.
3. **Practice responsibilities** — PMS read access; template approval; eligibility configuration;
   nominated contacts (§2 of the playbook); patient collection-notice update (APP 5).
4. **Meherr responsibilities** — nightly operation inside practice-set boundaries; guardrail
   monitoring with the playbook's stop rules; weekly reporting; no clinical claims in any
   patient-facing message (compliance linter enforced).
5. **What Meherr never does** — the venture brief's hard boundaries verbatim: never alters
   clinician follow-up plans, never labels an invitation medically necessary, never books without
   patient action, never uses clinical data for anything but eligibility inside the practice's
   rules.
6. **Data** — practice remains the health-information custodian; Meherr processes eligibility
   and event data solely to deliver the service; no identifiable data for model training;
   deletion/export per the retention policy (W33) on termination; breach notification per the
   NDB scheme. [Lawyer: data processing schedule.]
7. **Privacy & compliance** — Privacy Act/APPs; Spam Act consent basis and opt-out handling;
   Ahpra advertising guidelines apply to message content. [Lawyer: warranty allocation.]
8. **Fees** — pilot fee per docs/PRICING.md; credited on conversion; no other charges.
9. **Measurement & publicity** — pre-registered success criteria (playbook §6) recorded as a
   schedule before week 1; de-identified case study only with practice written approval.
10. **Liability & insurance** — [Lawyer: caps, mutual indemnities, Meherr PI/cyber cover;
    practice retains clinical responsibility for all care decisions.]
11. **Termination effects** — sends cease immediately; data export within 14 days; retention
    clock per policy; survival clauses. [Lawyer.]
