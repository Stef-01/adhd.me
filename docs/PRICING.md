# Meherr Pricing (W47, v1 — internal)

Flat platform fees only. **No percentage of clinical revenue, no per-referral payments, ever** —
this is a compliance posture (fee-splitting/inducement rules; see the venture brief §6 and the
research page §5), not just a pricing choice. A limited performance component may be tied to
*verified incremental attended appointments* (holdout-measured), never to referrals.

## Phase 1 — Continuity Yield (practice SaaS)

| Tier | Practice size | Annual fee (AUD, ex GST) | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo/small | 1–3 FTE GPs | $4,800 | one site, standard support |
| Standard | 4–7 FTE GPs | $9,600 | one site, weekly report walkthrough during first quarter |
| Group | 8–12 FTE GPs | $14,400–$18,000 | up to two sites |
| Multisite | 3+ sites | $25,000–$100,000 by scope | rollout support, quarterly business review |

- 12-week pilot: charged at the Standard-tier quarterly equivalent ($2,400), credited in full
  against year one on conversion. The pilot price exists so the practice has skin in the game;
  it is not a revenue line.
- Breakeven framing for the buyer (from the verified model): ~4 incremental attended visits/week
  practice-wide covers the Standard tier. The ROI calculator (W21) is the live version.
- Annual billing, 30-day termination for convenience after year one. No lock-in theatre.

## Later phases (indicative only — priced when built)

- Condition Yield modules (care-gap registers, in-panel routing): group contracts $25k–$100k/yr.
- Scope Yield network (credentialing + education + referral rails): GP subscription + PHN/health
  system/insurer contracts; specialists paid sessionally for governance. Never per-referral.

## Discounting rules

- No free pilots (see above). No perpetual discounts; time-boxed launch pricing only,
  documented here when used. Design-partner practices (first 3): 50% off year one in exchange
  for reference rights + case-study participation — that trade is explicit in the agreement.
