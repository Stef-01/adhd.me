# The night's root-cause analysis: tags, matching, transcripts (2026-09-29)

The founder's verdict at 12:10: the finder hallucinated "help at work with emotional regulation",
failed to list the providers who work on work and executive function, carries no bespoke tags for
what each provider is expert in, and the transcripts keep breaking. This is the multi-stage
analysis, each stage with its evidence, its cause, its fix and how it is proved, written as the
work proceeds. Everything here is checked against the record (`voice_calls`, `finder_searches`,
`qa/voice/runs`) rather than remembered.

## Stage 0. The incident, read from the record

The 12:08 call (`voice_calls`, seven questions, 89 s). The person's first words, as transcribed:
"a general cycle that will help me understand with stresses of workplace demands." Later answers
were transcribed as "什么?", "Nein." and "Gar". The interviewer asked who it was for (a question the
founder had already called useless), asked about telehealth twice, and said what it was about to
do twice. Its request: "Someone to support me with workplace stresses, general cycles, telehealth,
with a woman, and someone who has ADHD themselves." The model reader heard
`care:emotional-regulation`, `pref:telehealth-first`, `pref:woman-gp`, `pref:lived-experience`.
The list: Trisha Harris, Donna Italiano, Alice Bui, Lauren Poulos, Paula Garrido. Not one of the
seven ADHD coaches, whose own words are executive functioning, workplace and routines.

Four causes, each its own stage:

1. **Transcription is not pinned to English.** `audio.input.transcription` names the model and
   nothing else, so a short answer ("what?", "no", "nah") comes back in whatever language the
   model guesses: Chinese, German. The request then carries "general cycles". (Stage 1.)
2. **The request is written by the realtime model, not taken from the person.** It paraphrases,
   invents ("for a baby eight months old" for a mother asking for herself, in the text eval at
   12:13), drops facts ("diagnosed last year", earlier today) and merges answers. Every rule added
   to the prompt lowers the rate; none removes the class. (Stage 2.)
3. **The vocabulary has no home for what the person asked.** The lexicon reads nothing at all for
   "help with the stresses of workplace demands" or "help at work with focus and getting things
   done", and the model reader, forced to choose among keys that exist, picks the nearest:
   emotional regulation. The keys are a clinic's categories (assessment, titration, shared care,
   anxiety, depression), not the domains of a life (work and study, organisation and time,
   relationships and parenting, sleep, money and admin, emotions and rejection). The seven coaches
   who work on exactly this carry no tag that says so. (Stages 3 and 4.)
4. **A call's record was refused whole for a count**, and the refusal was silent. Fixed at 12:1x
   (migration 0012, `journal.failedLast` on `/api/health`); the 12:08 call is the first of the
   founder's to land. (Stage 5 hardens what remains.)

## Stage 1. Transcription fidelity

Evidence: "什么?", "Nein.", "Gar" in the 12:08 transcript for English answers.
Cause: no `language` on the transcription; no domain prompt.
Fix: `transcription: { model, language: "en", prompt }`, the prompt naming the words the finder
lives on (ADHD, telehealth, bulk billing, psychologist, GP, suburb names as spoken). An answer
that is one unclear word is asked again once ("Sorry, I didn't catch that").
Proof: the text eval, a scripted production call with short answers, and the drift check.

## Stage 2. The request is the person's own words

Evidence: 12:13 text eval, "for a baby eight months old"; 11:40 production call, "diagnosed last
year" dropped; 12:08, "general cycles".
Cause: composition delegated to the realtime model.
Fix: the client assembles the request from the person's turns: the first answer verbatim, every
free-text answer verbatim, and the yes/no questions mapped by rule ("Would you like someone who
has ADHD themselves?" + yes → "someone who has ADHD themselves"). The model's `show_matches`
request is kept in the transcript and used only when the person said nothing usable. The cost
question is dropped: one provider in 37 declares bulk billing, so no answer changes the list.
Proof: conversation unit tests, the text eval (whose `heard` keys come from the request), and
production calls.
Landed 12:40: `requestFromTurns` in `src/voice/conversation.ts`. Each answer is its own sentence
("…from the inside. Telehealth's fine, I'm in Marrickville."), a yes to the lived-experience
question is "someone who has ADHD themselves", a bare yes or no is nothing, a no followed by fewer
than six words is nothing ("no, English is fine"), a leading "but" or "and" after a yes or no is
dropped, and a turn that is not Latin text is dropped. The text eval's request cap (35 words,
written for the model's sentence) now guards a runaway at 250; a rambling persona's request is
165 words and reads correctly. The results card prints the first twenty words of any request
(O260, qa/design-qa.md); "Change what you said" holds them all. Three eval runs on the composed
request: 11/12 each, a different persona each time (docs/matching/VOICE-FINDER.md, row 11). The
third run's miss was the realtime model calling show_matches straight after its opening question,
with a request of its own ("I want help finding ADHD care"); the client now answers such a call as
not shown and tells the model to ask again, so no list is ever shown for words nobody said.
Found by the eval, fixed the same hour (O260): "a psychologist who's been diagnosed with ADHD
themself" read as an assessment ask, because "diagnosed" is an assessment cue and no lived cue
claimed it. The lexicon gains the clinician's own diagnosis, by reflexive ("diagnosed with ADHD
herself") and by the clinician noun's relative clause ("a GP who was diagnosed"), each claiming
its words; the model reader's meaning says the same; "someone who was diagnosed" is left out
because a carer says it of the person they are asking for.

## Stage 3. A tag schema from what people need

Written from the domains of a life, then from the care paths, then access, then who:
- Life domains: work and study; organisation, time and routines; money and admin; relationships,
  parenting and family; emotions and rejection; sleep; health habits; substances; driving and risk.
- Care paths: assessment; prescribing (start, continue, review); shared care; therapy; coaching;
  occupational therapy; exercise and physio; not medication.
- Alongside: anxiety, depression, trauma, autism and AuDHD, eating, perinatal, complex.
- Access: telehealth; in person and where; bulk billing or fees; NDIS; waits.
- Who: woman or man; has ADHD themselves; language; culture or background; ages seen.
Each tag: an id, a pill of at most two words, the question it is asked as, the words that reach
it, and the meaning the model reader is given. Each provider: the tags their own words support,
with the sentence that supports each, extracted and then read by a person. (Stage 3 file:
`src/matching/tags.ts`; provider tags: `src/demo/provider-tags.ts`.)

## Stage 4. Matching on tags, and the seven coaches

The reader's vocabulary becomes the tag set; the lexicon's cues come from the tags' words; the
ranking's care tier reads provider tags; pills on the rows and the profile are tags; the why
sentence names a tag. Proof: a patient simulation (Stage 6) in which "help at work with focus and
getting things done" lists the coaches first.

## Stage 5. Transcripts that cannot be lost

Per-turn persistence: each turn is sent as it happens and the call's row is upserted with the
turns so far, so a call that ends in a tunnel or a closed tab still has everything up to that
second; the final record completes it. Edge tests: hang-up mid-call, tab closed, a call past the
question cap, a second call in the same tab, a network error on one turn.
Landed 12:35: the voice stage mints the call's id when the call starts; after every turn it
reports the call so far as `stopped` under that id (`onCallProgress` → `trackVoiceCall`), the
store keeps one record per id and the table upserts by id (`resolution=merge-duplicates`), so the
end of the call completes the same row. A report lost to the network costs nothing but the next
turn's latency: every report carries every turn. Proof: the store test (a call reported again
replaces its earlier report), the tracker test (the id given is the id sent), and two e2e runs on
the scripted call: the whole call lands under one id with `revealed` and its search last, and a
call stopped after two answers keeps both. The question cap is 64 in the table since migration
0012; a second call in the same tab is a new component instance and a new id.

## Stage 6. Simulated patients

Thirty personas with nuanced needs, each naming the providers who should appear, run through the
lexicon reader and the model reader (cassettes) and the ranking; the gate is the share of personas
whose expected providers are in the first five. The cases the founder found are the first three.
