06
Product · the patient finder
built, and running.
The finder carries a person from “what do I need” to a booked first appointment inside one calm shell. No clinical language on the surface — the compliance linter fails the build if it appears.
Search-first and plain-language. Matching is keyed to clinician attributes — never to a patient's symptoms.
ADHD.ME
07
Product · the practice side
supply is the other
half of the problem.
Practices get demand matching inside their own rules, a record of which GP can do what, and a measurement layer that separates additional visits from the ones that would have happened anyway.
A randomised holdout arm runs by default, so the lift is measured rather than asserted. Every message, booking and opt-out sits on an immutable audit log.
Synthetic practice engine — 26 simulated weeks, 4,000 synthetic patients. No real patient data exists in this build.

08
Business model
flat fees. never a cut of care.
No percentage of clinical revenue and no per-referral payment, ever. That is a compliance posture — fee-splitting and inducement rules — not a pricing preference.
AUD, annual, ex GST. 12-week pilot at $2,400, credited in full against year one on conversion. Buyer breakeven sits at roughly four incremental attended visits per week, practice-wide.
