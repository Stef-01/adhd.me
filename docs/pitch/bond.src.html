The market is the people nobody found
two in three are not being treated.
Prevalence estimate: ~1.5 million Australians living with ADHD (2023 estimate; 2–6% of adults, 6–10% of children). Treatment: ~470,000 Australians dispensed ADHD medication in 2022–23 (AIHW). Dispensing is a lower bound on diagnosis — some diagnosed people are not medicated by choice — so the untreated share is the ceiling, not the count of undiagnosed.

MBS rebates at 1 August 2026. ADHD assessment and review bill as Level C and D, not Level B. Annual figure: one prolonged consult per working day × 46 weeks — conservative, and before any private gap. Patient saving is the out-of-pocket difference on a GP-led pathway in the states that now permit one.
